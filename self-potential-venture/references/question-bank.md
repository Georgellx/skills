# Question Bank

Ask one or two questions at a time in live coaching. Use this bank when the user asks for a worksheet or when you need more coverage.

## Phase 1: Why Now

- What made you start thinking about a side hustle or venture now?
- What risk are you trying to reduce: job loss, income drop, lack of autonomy, identity anxiety, or long-term growth?
- What result would count as progress in the next 30 days?
- What can you afford to lose: time, money, energy, public exposure, social pressure?
- What kinds of work do you refuse to do even if they seem profitable?

## Phase 2: Asset Inventory

- What have you repeatedly been responsible for at work?
- What projects have passed review, shipped, been accepted, or been used by others?
- What have you helped friends, colleagues, customers, or family solve?
- What do people ask you to explain, organize, fix, write, research, or judge?
- What templates, notes, workflows, checklists, scripts, or documents have you already made?
- Which certificates, tools, or methods do you actually know how to use in real situations?
- Which experiences were painful but taught you something others would rather avoid?

## Phase 3: Talent And Energy

- Which tasks feel obvious to you but difficult for others?
- When do you enter a focused state and lose track of time?
- What work gives you calm energy rather than only excitement?
- What do you learn even when nobody requires it?
- What kind of problems make you impatient because you can see a better way?
- What work drains you so much that you should not build a venture around it?

## Phase 4: Values And Fit

- What kind of life do you want the venture to support?
- Which matters more right now: cash, freedom, reputation, learning, stability, or expression?
- Do you prefer teaching, building tools, doing services, writing, consulting, selling, or curating?
- Do you want to work with individuals, teams, small businesses, students, creators, or operators?
- What would make a direction feel successful even before it earns much money?

## Phase 5: User Pain Translation

- Who is dealing with the same problem you have solved?
- What situation are they in when the problem becomes urgent?
- What do they use now instead of your possible solution?
- What does the problem cost them in time, money, mistakes, anxiety, or opportunity?
- Why would they seek help from a person like you?
- What would make them ignore your offer?

## Phase 6: Direction Hypothesis

For each candidate, ask:

- What specific audience would this serve first?
- What painful moment would your content or offer address?
- What asset of yours gives you unfair familiarity with this problem?
- What is the smallest useful thing you could provide?
- What proof would make this direction more credible?
- What would be the cheapest test?

## Phase 7: Validation

- Who can you interview without pretending to sell?
- What past behavior questions will reveal real demand?
- What content can you publish to test whether people care?
- What commitment would count as real signal: reply, save, share, intro, data, trial, payment, or waitlist?
- What result would make you continue?
- What result would make you stop or narrow the idea?

## Rescue Questions For Stuck Users

- If none of this could make money, which problem would you still want to understand better?
- If you had to help someone only one step behind you, who would that be?
- If your career disappeared tomorrow, which skills would remain portable?
- What have you survived, fixed, or figured out that your past self would have paid to know?
- What do your files, notes, folders, screenshots, and templates prove you keep returning to?
