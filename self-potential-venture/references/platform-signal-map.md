# Platform Signal Map

Use this reference to decide which public channel or delivery platform is plausible for a user's first validation step. This is not a platform-growth guarantee.

## Evidence Boundary

Public search results and articles show visible packaging patterns, not hidden conversion data. Platform choice must be validated with small posts, lead magnets, comments, saves, private messages, or explicit purchase intent.

## Platform: Xiaohongshu / Rednote

Useful for:

- consumer-like professional pain points
- visual checklists, carousels, before/after examples
- low-ticket资料包, templates, prompt packs, notebooks, creator workflows

Public signals:

- Virtual-material articles describe demand for PPT, resume, study, office, and skill-related资料包.
- Narrow pain points matter more than giant generic packs.
- Good packaging often uses scenario-specific titles, screenshots, and "what you get" lists.

Best validation:

- 5 carousel posts around one pain
- one free checklist in comments/private message
- observe saves, comments, DMs, and repeated wording

Risk:

- easy to drift into broad "副业赚钱" claims
- platform examples may exaggerate earnings
- business/enterprise audiences may be thinner than consumer/professional audiences

## Platform: Zhihu

Useful for:

- reasoning-heavy domains
- professional judgment, teardown, decision criteria
- B2B/product/management/AI topics where readers search for explanations

Public signals:

- Articles can carry long-form frameworks, case analysis, and productized-service reasoning.
- Q&A-style topics reveal pains, misconceptions, and purchase objections.

Best validation:

- one long answer to a concrete problem
- one "how to judge X" article
- one case-based teardown with anonymized details

Risk:

- likes do not equal buying intent
- broad self-improvement topics are noisy
- strong claims get challenged

## Platform: Bilibili

Useful for:

- explainers, walkthroughs, product demos, AI workflow demonstrations
- long-form teaching and screen recordings
- building trust through showing how the method works

Public signals:

- Search-visible AI副业 and AI product manager videos often use "教程/资料/工具/提示词" as hooks.
- Comment or pinned material links are commonly used as lead magnets.

Best validation:

- 8-15 minute teardown video
- screen-recorded template walkthrough
- "before/after report generation" demo

Risk:

- video production takes more energy
- audience may come for free learning, not paid consulting
- broad AI副业 claims are crowded

## Platform: WeChat Official Account / Newsletter

Useful for:

- trust-building in professional topics
- long-form serialized frameworks
- distribution through existing relationships or groups

Best validation:

- 3-5 article series around one problem
- share into relevant groups or contacts
- collect replies and private questions

Risk:

- weak discovery without existing distribution
- slow feedback loop

## Platform: Knowledge Planet / Paid Community

Useful for:

- ongoing updates, Q&A, case accumulation, peer discussion
- topics where members need repeated support

Public signals:

- Knowledge-community guides emphasize layered content: foundational knowledge, domain depth, and cross-domain connection.

Best validation before launching:

- repeated reader questions
- several free or low-ticket artifacts
- clear recurring benefit

Risk:

- high delivery burden
- not a good first product for users without audience

## Platform: Gumroad / Digital Product Marketplaces

Useful for:

- templates, prompt packs, Notion systems, small courses, digital downloads
- international/English-facing products or creator audiences

Public signals:

- Public stories show free templates can build an email/audience base before paid templates.
- Gumroad category pages show many free and paid digital products, but marketplace presence does not prove demand for a new seller.

Best validation:

- free product download
- email capture
- small paid upgrade

Risk:

- needs distribution outside the marketplace
- many categories are crowded

## Platform: Notion / Feishu / Airtable Template Ecosystems

Useful for:

- workflow-heavy expertise
- project management, knowledge base, SOP, risk tracking, content calendars

Public signals:

- Notion, Feishu, Smartsheet, and similar ecosystems expose templates for project management, risk tracking, SOP, content planning, and dashboards.

Best validation:

- publish a free template
- pair it with a usage guide
- ask users what they changed or still could not solve

Risk:

- template usefulness depends on the user's actual process maturity
- templates alone often fail when the real problem is judgment or change management

## Platform Choice Rules

- If the user's first asset is judgment, start with teardown content.
- If the user's first asset is a reusable document, start with a template/checklist.
- If the user's first asset is a workflow, start with a demo or guide.
- If the user's first asset is audience trust, consider community or higher-ticket consulting.
- If there is no audience and cases are confidential, start with composite examples and problem posts.
