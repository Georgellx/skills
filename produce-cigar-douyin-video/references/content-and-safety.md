# Cigar Editorial and Safety Contract

## Editorial voice

Write like an experienced hobbyist explaining one useful detail to a friend:

- lead with the mistake, consequence, contrast, or practical answer;
- use short spoken sentences and concrete verbs;
- explain one causal chain at a time;
- distinguish personal practice from broadly supported guidance;
- avoid “首先、其次、最后”, “值得注意的是”, “综上所述”, exaggerated certainty, and generic motivational endings;
- read the script aloud and revise phrases that sound written rather than spoken.

Do not pad duration. Let the approved content determine the final audio length.

## Fact handling

Tag every material statement as one of:

- `verified_fact` with a recorded source;
- `stable_craft_consensus` with a reputable reference;
- `personal_practice` stated as experience rather than a universal rule;
- `uncertain` and removed or presented with appropriate qualification.

Do not invent precise temperatures, humidity, resting periods, chemical explanations, rankings, or health effects. Preserve meaningful ranges and conditions from sources.

## Adult and health framing

- Address adults only.
- Keep the video educational, not promotional.
- Do not show minors, youth-coded characters, school settings, or youth-oriented language.
- Do not show smoking, inhalation, a lit cigar, ignition, purchase guidance, prices, discounts, sellers, or calls to buy by default.
- Avoid health, status, relaxation, success, masculinity, or social-benefit claims.
- Include the persistent notice: `视频内容仅为知识科普，吸烟有害健康，请尽早戒烟；未成年人禁止吸烟。`

If the topic or requested visual would conflict with applicable law, platform policy, or active workspace rules, explain the issue and propose a safer educational treatment before production.

## Originality and sources

Use web research to verify and enrich facts, not to imitate a creator's copyrighted expression. Never copy another video's script, title sequence, shot order, footage, music, or distinctive wording.

The approved local reference may be decomposed deeply for style: timing, hierarchy, palette, composition, subtitle behavior, movement density, and transition grammar. Reproduce those abstract production parameters while creating original footage, wording, and audio.
