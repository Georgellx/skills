# White Editorial v2

Use editorial whitespace, fine rules, strong black typography, restrained yellow marking, and a proportionate portrait window. Let the speaker remain the emotional center while information receives its own reading space.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#F5F5EF`
- Ink: `#11110F`
- Muted text: `#6F6D66`
- Rule: `#D8D6CC`
- Marker: `#F2D431`
- Portrait border: `#DDDCD3`
- Page safe area: 68px sides, 72px top, 250px bottom
- Caption: white bold text, black outline, transparent background

## Scene families

- Hook: full portrait or large portrait plus a direct question.
- Explanation: editorial information field plus a separate portrait window.
- Sequence: semantic list or timeline revealed in spoken order.
- Turn: one controlled high-intensity motion passage.
- CTA: full portrait question followed by a quiet closing page.

Recalculate portrait crop, text quantity, and animation timing for every source and script. Use semantic metadata only; omit decorative labels that do not add information.
